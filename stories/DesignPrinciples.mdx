## Guidelines

1. **Monochrome first.** Hierarchy comes from tone, weight, size and space. Error is the only semantic color, reserved for errors and destructive actions.
2. **Straight geometry.** No rounded corners. radius-full is only for Avatar round, Radio and the Slider thumb.
3. **One primary action per view.** Use one primary Button per view or section; other actions are secondary, tertiary or links.
4. **Quiet structure.** Separate content with spacing first, then with 1 px border-default dividers. Controls use border-strong.
5. **Same names everywhere.** Components, props and tokens have the same names in Figma, React and the component contract.
6. **Accessible by default.** Every control has a visible label or an accessible name, a visible focus state and at least 3:1 contrast against its background.

</section>

<section>

## Accessibility

Contrast and focus rules above apply to every component; check custom content with the a11y panel.
