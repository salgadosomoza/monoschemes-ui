# Composer

Where people write to the agent: a textarea that grows, attachments and a bar with attach, voice and send. Suggestions sits above it after an answer.

## When to use

Use the Composer at the end of the thread for the next message.

## Guidelines

empty (send disabled), focused (2 px border/focused), filled, busy (send becomes Stop with player-stop) and voice. showHint adds a caption line, for example «Enter to send, Shift + Enter for a new line».

The microphone starts voice mode; then people talk without holding anything. Agent/Mark shows listening and speaking, and every word is written into the same thread. End voice (or Esc) goes back to the keyboard.

</section>

<section>

## Accessibility

The textarea has a visible or aria label. Enter sends and Shift + Enter adds a line. Attachments are listed as removable Tags. After sending, focus stays in the Composer.

</section>
